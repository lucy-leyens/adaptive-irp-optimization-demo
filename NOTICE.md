# Portfolio / attribution notice

This repository is an **independent, simplified portfolio implementation** inspired by research carried out by me, Lucy Z. Leyen,  during her Master's thesis at Fraunhofer IKS.

It intentionally does **not** contain the original Fraunhofer research codebase. The implementation in this repository was created separately for demonstration and recruitment purposes. The public demo also omits selected research details and components of the full thesis framework.

Research context and original thesis work: Lucy Z. Leyens, *Large-Scale Optimization for the Inventory Routing Problem*, RWTH Aachen University / Fraunhofer IKS, 2026.
